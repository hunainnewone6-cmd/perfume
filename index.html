The House

Crafted with Intention

Every Maison Néra fragrance is composed slowly, as an act of attention. Rare materials are selected for their character rather than their fame, blended in small silent batches, and then left to rest until the composition finds its voice — sometimes for a season, occasionally for a year.

I.

Selection & Sourcing

Materials are chosen from the world’s great growing regions and evaluated by nose, not by price list.

II.

The Blend

Each formula is composed in our Grasse atelier and tested on skin for weeks before it is accepted.

III.

Refinement & Rest

Bottles are macerated in darkness and cold, then refined by hand until the fragrance wears as it was intended.

“A fragrance is not simply worn. It is remembered.”
— Maison Néra
